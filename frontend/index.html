COMMON QUESTIONS

FAQ

What streams are available?

The +2 Science program has Physical Group and Biology Group.

What subjects are in Physical Group?

Physics, Chemistry and Computer Science and Information Technology, along with Nepali, English and Mathematics.

What subjects are in Biology Group?

Physics, Chemistry and Biology, along with Nepali, English and Mathematics.

Is Computer Science available?

Yes. Computer Science and Information Technology is available in Physical Group.

What credit courses are available?

Examples include Nepali Reading, English Reading, Public Speaking and Python Programming.
